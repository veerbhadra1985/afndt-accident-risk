PUBLISHING THE CORRECTED REPOSITORY AS v2.3
===========================================

WHY v2.3 AND NOT v2.2 AGAIN
The v2.2 tag already exists and points at the flattened tree. If Zenodo has
already archived it, that DOI is permanent and cannot be withdrawn - the only
remedy is to publish a newer version, which then becomes what the concept DOI
resolves to. Tagging v2.3 works whether or not Zenodo saw v2.2, and avoids
deleting anything. Leave v2.1 and v2.2 where they are.

WHAT THIS FOLDER IS
The repository as it should be: 251 files in four directories, all 187 predicted
probability vectors, results/protocol/protocol_runs.json restored, plus the four
figure scripts and the density simulation. All four scripts were run inside this
exact tree and reproduce the published values.

THE DOI IN THE PAPER DOES NOT CHANGE
10.5281/zenodo.22932569 is the concept DOI and always resolves to the newest
version, so it will point at v2.3 once published.
